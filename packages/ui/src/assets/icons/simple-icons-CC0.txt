Site brand marks in ./site were vendored from Simple Icons 13.0.0.

Provenance: https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/tree/13.0.0
Local adaptation: preserve upstream path geometry and adjust fills for the two theme surfaces as described below.
License: CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain dedication)
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Each file keeps the upstream path geometry verbatim. One rule resolves the fill
once, at vendoring time: the mark must clear 3.1:1 against both theme surfaces,
because --surface-base is #FFFFFF in light and #242424 in dark and a single
static fill is drawn on either. An official color already inside that range is
used unchanged; a chromatic brand outside it has its HSL lightness moved just
far enough to re-enter the range, which preserves the recognizable hue. Brands
whose official mark is drawn in black or near-black are the exception: their
identity is the silhouette, so they are pinned to currentColor and follow the
surrounding text color rather than being recolored.

Trademarks remain the property of their owners; these marks identify the
referenced site only.
